Delete your account

Finance Tracker · HabitAroha

You can delete your account and its data at any time. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone, so export anything you want to keep first.

Finance Tracker

From inside the app

Open Settings → Account → Delete My Account, then confirm. You will be asked to re-enter your password before the deletion runs.

If you cannot sign in

Email support@lyvorianstudio.co.in from the address registered on the account, with the subject Account deletion and your username in the message. Requests are actioned manually. We reply from the same address; we will not ask you for your password.

What is deleted

  • Your username, password hash and email address
  • All expenses, income, budgets, goals, bills, accounts and categories
  • Trading journal entries, if you used that feature
  • Your settings and preferences
  • Active sessions — you are signed out everywhere

What is not deleted by that action

  • Backups already emailed to you. Those are in your own mailbox and only you can delete them.
  • Backup files you downloaded to your own device.
  • Payment records. Razorpay retains transaction records independently, as its own payment records; we do not control that retention.

HabitAroha

HabitAroha stores habit content on your device. These are separate actions and doing one does not do the others:

  • Delete local data — Settings → Delete account, inside the app.
  • Delete Google Drive backups — you are asked about this during in-app deletion if you ever switched Drive backup on. You can also remove them from your own Drive.
  • Cancel a subscription — done in Google Play, not in the app. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.
  • Uninstalling the app removes its local data, but does not remove Drive backups or cancel a subscription.

Subscriptions

Deleting an account does not automatically cancel an active subscription. Cancel it first — Finance Tracker Pro through the payment provider used at purchase, HabitAroha through Google Play.

How we confirm it is you

In-app deletion re-checks your password. Email requests must come from the address registered on the account. If neither is possible we cannot safely verify ownership and will not delete the account, because doing so on an unverified request would let someone else destroy your data.
