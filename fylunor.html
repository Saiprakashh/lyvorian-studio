Product № 3

Fylunor

A document workspace that keeps your files where they belong — on your own device.

Web — in development
Not released yet. Fylunor is still being built. There is nothing to open or download today — this page describes what it does, not what you can use.

Most document tools work by sending your file somewhere. You upload a PDF to compress it, or to sign it, or to pull the text out of a scan — and your document is now on a server you do not control. Fylunor is built the other way round: the work happens on your device.

Scanning, text recognition, page cleanup, compression, redaction, password protection and conversion all run inside the app. Some of those tools are several megabytes of code precisely so that your files never have to leave.

What it does

  • Scan and clean upCapture a document, straighten it, and tidy the page without it ever being uploaded.
  • Read text from scansOptical character recognition runs locally, so the page never leaves to be read.
  • A full PDF toolkitCompress, convert, protect with a password, redact and sign — all on your device.
  • Editors for real workDocuments, spreadsheets, invoices and resumes, alongside your scans and images.
  • The VaultFiles encrypted on your device with AES-256-GCM, using a key derived from your passphrase.
  • Backup you ownOptional backup to your own Google Drive — off until you turn it on.

Where your documents actually are

Everything you create or import — documents, spreadsheets, scans, PDFs, images, invoices, resumes — is stored on your device, in the app's own storage. It is not sent to us, and there is nowhere in our systems to put it.

  • No file is uploaded to us to be processed
  • Backup, if you enable it, goes to your own Google Drive — not to a Lyvorian server
  • Fylunor asks Google for the narrowest Drive permission available: access only to files the app itself created
  • It cannot see, read or list anything else in your Drive, including files made with other apps
  • Signing in is optional — Fylunor works fully without it
  • No tracking, and no data selling

The Vault, and one consequence worth stating

Files you put in the Vault are encrypted on your device with AES-256-GCM, using a key derived from your passphrase. The key exists only in memory while the Vault is open, and the passphrase itself is never stored anywhere — not by us, and not on your device.

That has a consequence worth saying plainly: if you forget the passphrase, nobody can recover those files — not you, and not us. Vault files are also excluded from Drive backup entirely, and on Android the screen is protected from screenshots and screen recording while the Vault is open.

What it will cost

Fylunor will have a free tier and a Pro subscription. Pricing is not settled yet, so there is no number here to quote — when it is decided it will appear on this page and in the app before anything is charged.

Privacy

Lyvorian runs exactly one small service for Fylunor, and all it can answer is whether an account has an active Pro subscription. The full Fylunor privacy policy spells out what is stored, what leaves your device, and what you can switch off — every claim in it checked against the code rather than written from intention.

How it's built

Built by Lyvorian Studio with the same priorities as Finance Tracker and HabitAroha: clarity, privacy, and nothing shipped until it holds up on real hardware rather than on the development machine. What that rule costs in practice — including the feature that got deleted instead of optimised — is in Built the hard way, and where Fylunor sits in the queue is on the roadmap.
