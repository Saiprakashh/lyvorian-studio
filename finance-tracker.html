Finance Tracker

All of your money in one clean app — salary, groceries, bills, goals. Not a trading terminal, not a spreadsheet with extra steps.

Web — live now Android — coming soon
Open Finance Tracker

What it does

  • Expenses & incomeEvery transaction categorised, so you can see where the month actually went.
  • BudgetsSet what a category should cost and watch it against what it really costs.
  • GoalsSavings targets with progress you can see, not just a number in your head.
  • BillsUpcoming bills and reminders, so nothing arrives as a surprise.
  • Bill scanningPoint it at a bill instead of typing the whole thing in by hand.
  • UPI auto-detectUPI payments recognised and filed without manual entry.

What it costs

Free to use. A Pro tier exists for the people who want more — no funding round to chase, no growth hacks, so the app doesn't need to squeeze you to survive.

How it's built

By one person, by hand, and measured on a real phone rather than assumed. The performance hunt that halved the app's CPU use — and the pretty feature that got deleted instead of optimised — is written up in Built the hard way.
