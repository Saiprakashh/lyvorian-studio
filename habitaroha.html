HabitAroha

Build calm, consistent habits. Works offline. Your data stays yours.

Android — in testing
Not released yet. V1 is built and running on real devices, going through a full test pass before the Play Store listing goes up. There's nothing to download today.

HabitAroha is a habit tracker for people who want steady progress rather than noisy streak pressure. It works completely offline, keeps your data encrypted on your own device, and never asks you to feel bad about a missed day.

What it does

  • Eight habit typesCheck-ins, counts, timed sessions, measurements, checklists, and habits you want to reduce.
  • Schedules that fitDaily, weekly, times-per-week, every-few-days, or your own custom dates.
  • See it clearlyMonth, week and year calendar views, plus a monthly grid with frozen headers.
  • Insights you can act onYour strongest weekday, when in the day you finish, and a consistency heatmap.
  • Kind by designPlanned rest days never count as missed, and vacation mode protects your streaks.
  • Reminders that respect youPer-habit times, morning summary, evening review, and quiet hours.

Your data, genuinely yours

  • Encrypted on your device with AES-256
  • Encrypted backups you can restore with a recovery key
  • Optional backup to your own Google Drive — off unless you turn it on
  • Export to CSV, Excel, PDF or a shareable progress image
  • Download everything you have stored, any time
  • PIN and biometric lock

What it costs

The free plan covers up to seven active habits with every tracking feature, exports and backups included — supported by a small banner ad below your habit list, never on a private note, a running timer or a sign-in screen. Founder Pro lifts the habit limit, removes advertisements, and supports a small independent studio.
